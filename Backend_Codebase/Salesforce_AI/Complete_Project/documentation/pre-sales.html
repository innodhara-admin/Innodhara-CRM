Sales process update

What happens before a lead becomes real

A school is now called and qualified before the team starts working it. This is the new step, start to finish, following one school the whole way.

The gap we were filling

Until now every lead we created was treated as already confirmed — we could book meetings and start follow-ups from the moment it existed. In practice somebody has to phone the school first and find out whether there is any interest at all.

Two possible outcomes

Whatever Priya finds out on the phone, she records it in one tap.

Positive response

She marks it Open

She adds a short note saying what the school said. The whole sales team is told, and the school becomes a normal live opportunity.

No interest

She marks it Not interested

Nothing is deleted. The school stays on record with the reason, and can be picked up again later when the timing is better.

Who can open a lead

This is the one place where ownership matters more than seniority. Priya made the calls, so only she knows what was said.

The moment it opens, everyone hears

Two things happen automatically, so nobody has to go looking for new work.

Seeing the journey at a glance

Inside a school there is now a strip showing everywhere it has been, with the date and the note attached to each step. No more guessing how a lead reached its current state.

Bulk upload

The upload file can now carry an owner for each school, so a list of fifty schools can be split across the agents in one go — each one lands in cold calling with the right person notified.

Mistyped names are reported, not guessed

If a name in the file does not match one of our agents, that row is listed as a failure rather than quietly assigned to whoever did the upload. A lead assigned to the wrong person is worse than a row you have to fix, because nobody would ever find out.
